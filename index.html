If you hire me

My first 90 days.

How I'd start in an Associate PM, BA or Scrum role, based on how I've onboarded onto delivery teams before.

30days

Listen & learn

  • Map stakeholders, decision-makers and how they like to communicate
  • Learn the delivery cadence, tools and reporting rhythm
  • Review the backlog, the RAID log and current risks
  • Ship small wins inside current sprints
60days

Own & stabilise

  • Take ownership of ceremonies or one workstream
  • Set up a clear status report (Power BI or Confluence)
  • Tighten how issues are triaged and escalated
  • Build KT material to cut onboarding time
90days

Improve & scale

  • Propose improvements to the process based on data
  • Track delivery metrics: velocity, cycle time, defect trends
  • Build trust by keeping commitments predictable
  • Agree on the next level of ownership
